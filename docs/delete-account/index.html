Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Scripta account and the data attached to it. It applies to the Scripta app (io.marginalia.app), published by Scripta App.

From inside the app

The fastest way, and it takes effect immediately:

  1. Open Scripta and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is closed straight away and you are signed out on that device.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, write to support@get-scripta.app from the email address of the account, asking us to delete it. Sending it from that address is how we confirm the account is yours. We reply within 30 days.

no forms, no phone calls — one email is enough

What gets deleted

Deleted Your account and sign-in credentials; your profile (name, username, photo); your books, highlights and notes; your posts, comments, reviews and messages; images you uploaded; and the device tokens used to send you notifications.
Kept Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous crash reports, which carry no account data, may remain in our diagnostics for the ordinary retention period.

Your data is removed from our live systems at once. Copies that remain in routine encrypted backups are erased as those backups rotate out.

Data that never leaves your phone

Health signals (steps, sleep, workouts, cycle phase) and calendar events are read on the device to choose which of your saved highlights to show, and are never uploaded. Nothing needs deleting on our side, because we never received it. Revoking the permission in your system settings stops the reading immediately.
