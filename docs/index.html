How it works

Three steps. Zero effort.

01

Bring your highlights in

Sign in to your Amazon account inside the app and your Kindle highlights import themselves. Years of reading, back in one tap.

02

One line finds you every morning

A widget on your home screen serves one highlight a day — chosen to fit the day you're having. ✱ It's a little eerie how often it fits.

03

Share the ones worth sharing

Reading circles with your friends: post your best lines, review books, vote the book of the month, defend your terrible opinions.
