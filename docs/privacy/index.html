Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Marginalia is a personal reading app. Your highlights are yours. We collect only what is strictly necessary to make the app work, we never sell your data, and we never will.

This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how to delete everything with one tap.
🔒
No tracking We use no advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.
🚫
Not sold Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers.
🗑
Delete anytime One tap in Settings deletes your account and all data.
📴
Offline-first Your highlights are stored on-device. Network is optional.

04 Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data on the following legal bases:

Contract (Art. 6.1.b) — email address and highlights are necessary to provide the service you signed up for.

Consent (Art. 6.1.a & Art. 9.2.a) — health data (steps, workouts, cycle phase) is only processed if you explicitly grant HealthKit permission on your device. You can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f) — city-level weather via Open-Meteo to provide contextual widget content, with no persistent identifier attached.

05 Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account), all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers within 30 days, including your highlights, profile, and Jam activity.

On-device data (Isar database) is removed immediately when you uninstall the app.

07 Children

Marginalia is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

08 Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you within the app before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
